# CollageX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

## Overview

CollageX is an app for making photo collages and stories on your iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data CollageX processes, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Your projects and the photos you add to them are stored on your device. CollageX has no account system, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Data the App Processes

### Photos You Choose

When you add photos to a project, CollageX uses the system photo picker. The app only receives the photos you select and cannot see the rest of your photo library, so it does not ask for photo library read access.

Selected photos are copied into the app's private storage on your device so your projects keep working even if the original is later edited or deleted in Photos. The app reads only what it needs to display and edit a photo, such as its dimensions and orientation. These copies are never uploaded.

### Your Projects

Layouts, templates, text, stickers, fonts in use, and thumbnails for your projects are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are protected by iOS data protection.

### Save to Photos

When you save a finished project, CollageX asks for **add-only** photo library access. This permission lets the app add new images to Photos. It does not let the app read or change your existing photos. The permission is optional. If you decline, you can still share your project.

Exported images are newly rendered by the app and do not include EXIF metadata such as location or camera details from the original photos.

### Sharing

When you tap Share, CollageX opens the iOS share sheet. The exported image goes only to the destination you choose, and that destination handles it under its own privacy policy.

### Font Downloads

Some fonts are downloaded on demand when you first use them. They are delivered through Apple's hosted Background Assets service, so the download request is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The request does not include your projects or photos.

## Third-Party Services

Future versions of CollageX may add third-party services, such as crash diagnostics, usage analytics, or advertising. Before such a version is released, we will update this policy to describe each service, the data it receives, why, and how you can control it, and we will update the app's privacy details on the App Store. Where the law or Apple's rules require your permission, for example for tracking, the app will ask for it first.

These services will not receive your projects or the photos you add to them.

## Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your projects and the photos in them are stored only on your device:

- Deleting a project in the app removes its project files, including the photo copies stored in that project.
- Uninstalling CollageX removes all of its data from your device.
- Images you saved to Photos and the original photos in your library are managed by iOS and are not affected by deleting a project or uninstalling the app.
- If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include CollageX data in those backups according to your device settings. Apple handles those backups.

## Permissions

- **Add to Photos (optional):** saves finished projects to your photo library. The app cannot read your existing photos.

CollageX does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

## Children's Privacy

CollageX is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
