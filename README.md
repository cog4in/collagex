# CollageX Public Privacy Policy and Support

This repository contains the public privacy policy and support pages for CollageX. It contains no app source code.

Public URLs (GitHub Pages):

- Main page: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagex/`
- English privacy policy: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagex/docs/privacy-policy-en`
- Chinese privacy policy: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagex/docs/privacy-policy-zh`
- Support page: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagex/docs/support.html` (`?lang=en` or `?lang=zh` selects the language)

Publication requirements:

- Serve these files from a public HTTPS URL without login, payment, or region restrictions.
- Keep the English and Chinese policies in sync, and update the "Last updated" date on every material change.
- Keep the policy aligned with the app's actual data handling, its privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`), and the App Store privacy label.
- Before releasing a version that adds a third-party SDK (for example Firebase or an ad network), add a section for each service to both policies and update the privacy manifest and App Store privacy label.
- The app's About page (Settings > About CollageX) links to the privacy policy and support URLs above; changing a URL requires an app update.
- Contact email: `cog4init@gmail.com`.
